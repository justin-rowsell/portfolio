Far Afield is where I keep short notes on what I'm building, learning, and still figuring out — AI tutoring, climate, building solo, CTO work, and wherever else curiosity takes me.

The bar is low on purpose. Expect quick notes, not polished essays: what I learned this week, why I killed an idea, something I'm still confused about. Every post is dated and tagged, and they all stay up — including the early takes I'll probably disagree with later.

If you'd like new posts by email, subscribe below. There's also an [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
